# Five-minute recording script

Deadline: October 17, 2026, 7:00 AM Pacific Time.

## Preflight

Run the checks in README. Start the app on port 3000. Use a fresh local dataset by stopping the app and moving `.data` to a backup location if necessary. Open the app and keep the private reviewer passcode off camera. Keep the demo banner visible. If live AI is part of the recording, configure and test `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` before recording; never show the environment file. Import only approved public resources if USM supplies them. Otherwise describe all resources as fictional samples.

## 0:00-0:35 - The problem and purpose

“Each graduating SSA leadership team takes experience with them. Chapters repeat work and miss opportunities to serve together. SSA Network gives Sikh students a shared home for seva, knowledge, and recognition.” Show the overview and Arena. State that all demonstration records are fictional.

## 0:35-1:25 - A need becomes action

Enter as a student, open the winter care-kit community request, and show completion criteria, grant conditions, and transparent chapter recommendations. Point out that requests can come from community nonprofits or universities as well as Gurdwaras. Switch to the University of Washington fictional officer. Accept for the chapter. Open My Challenges.

## 1:25-2:10 - Proof, not promises

Submit this fictional summary: “Our chapter assembled and distributed 40 winter care kits with the requesting organization.” Impact: “40 neighbors received winter supplies; our partner confirmed distribution.” Evidence: “Demo evidence: 40-kit item list, partner confirmation, and $150 receipt summary. Fictional evidence used only for this demonstration.” Participants: 12. Total volunteer-hours: 24. Show the pending status and unchanged leaderboard.

## 2:10-3:05 - Human trust and recognition

Switch to USM reviewer; hide the passcode entry from the recording. Open the review desk. Compare evidence to the completion criteria. Set verified hours to 20, show the score preview and capped formula, confirm the grant eligibility, choose approval, enter reviewer feedback, and confirm the human decision. Show the updated leaderboard and chapter ledger. Say: “Approval atomically records one point award, one eligible grant record, and one verified achievement post. Repeating the request cannot duplicate these records. Grant records do not move money.”

## 3:05-3:35 - A national community

Open National Feed. Show the verified achievement. Switch to student and give Shabash. Refresh to show persistence. Explain that there are no public comments, repeated clicks toggle rather than inflate counts, and proposed captions require human moderation.

## 3:35-4:25 - Knowledge that stays

Open Toolkit, search “langar,” and open the guide. Clearly call it a fictional sample. If Claude is configured, ask Sahayak “How do we organize a langar seva event?” Show the actionable answer and click a retrieved-source citation. Explain that answers distinguish Toolkit guidance from general suggestions and that AI never approves work or commits money. If AI is not configured, show the honest setup state and say the integration needs a server-side key; do not claim an AI answer was generated.

## 4:25-5:00 - From message to movement

Optional, if Claude is configured and tested: reviewer opens Community requests and pastes “Our demo community center in Seattle needs 8 volunteers on November 15, 2026 to assemble 40 winter care kits.” Show editable extraction and missing-information warnings. Do not publish without confirming the unknown completion and grant details. Close: “USM can publish real needs, chapters can collaborate and prove their work, and the whole sangat can learn from what comes next.”

## Known demonstration limits

Live Supabase, Vercel, and Claude must be validated with your accounts. Sample resources are not official USM policy. Scoring requires USM approval. Evidence uses links, not binary uploads. No money is transferred, and no video is generated by this project.
